Thu, 15 Oct 26

#### **The Content Crisis: Why Current Approaches Are Failing**

* AI adoption is near-universal (93% of marketers), but adaptation is lagging  
* Most teams are using AI to go faster, not to rethink direction  
* Three converging trends creating a structural problem:  
  * 25% of consumers now turn to AI platforms first (more than brand websites) for purchase decisions  
  * Organic search clicks down 42% across one portfolio, correlated with Google AI Overviews  
  * 35% of web pages now show significant AI authorship, flooding supply  
* Supply/demand inversion: content supply exploding, audience attention shrinking  
  * Audiences increasingly use AI for the same jobs content used to do

#### **The AI-Native Content Playbook: Five Steps**

* **Step 1: Original Inputs.** Human insights, data, and ideas AI cannot supply on its own  
  * Expert interviews, original research, proprietary frameworks, lived experience, customer conversations  
  * If AI can originate it without human input, stop making it  
  * Example: 34-question State of AI for Business survey, 2,100 respondents, 100,000+ data points, 90%+ with qualitative written answers  
* **Step 2: Anchor Assets.** Original inputs become flagship content pieces  
  * Research reports, podcast episodes, webinars, in-depth guides  
  * Build reusable AI skills trained on editorial standards, voice, and process  
  * State of AI report: draft-to-design-ready now takes \~1 week vs. 4+ weeks before  
* **Step 3: Content Portfolio.** Each anchor asset fans out across formats, channels, and teams  
  * Blog posts, social, video clips, sales enablement, training resources, activation briefs  
  * Custom skills per team (e.g., sales vs. customer success activation briefs with different KPIs)  
  * Distribution increasingly automated: AI connected to CMS/CRM to upload and post  
* **Step 4: Living Knowledge.** Content portfolio becomes a queryable, always-on knowledge base  
  * Example: 246-episode podcast archive with AI layered over it, queryable in seconds  
  * Stored in shared repos (e.g., Google Drive \+ Gemini), connected via integrations and MCPs  
  * AI Score (SmartRx product) now collects real-time benchmark data, replacing the annual survey cycle  
* **Step 5: Agentic Engine.** Agents connect to living knowledge to monitor, recommend, and create  
  * Agents given ongoing roles and boundaries to function as always-on content specialists  
  * Tasks: content monitoring, strategy recommendations, creation, distribution, performance analysis  
  * Direction of travel: agents as content team members, managed by human content professionals

#### **Bigger-Picture Takeaways**

* Getting close to people is now a core job requirement: the only defensible data is what’s in people’s heads  
* If AI can create it without meaningful human input, stop creating it; spend that time sourcing original inputs  
* Shift mental model from individual assets to compounding systems  
  * Content professionals are becoming system managers and database maintainers, not just creators  
* Agents will function as specialist content employees, connected to the living knowledge base  
* Human content professionals remain invaluable as the originators of inputs AI cannot replicate
