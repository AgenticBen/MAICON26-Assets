# **MAICON Zapier presentation**

Thu, 15 Oct 26

#### **Dan Slagen’s Framing (Zapier CMO)**

* No one in the room is “behind in AI”: labs, consultants, and influencers drive that narrative  
* Real question: is the business in better shape than a year ago?  
  * Too many teams adopting AI without measurable impact  
  * One CEO extreme: pulled AI entirely to refocus on customers  
* Four phases of AI adoption: creation, roles/functions, dev tools, agentic  
  * Fewest people reaching agentic stage, most hype concentrated there  
  * A chasm exists between early adopters and the majority still getting started  
* Core belief: Jensen Huang’s framing: AI is opportunity, not a layoff tool  
  * Companies using AI for headcount cuts “have run out of creative ideas”

#### **The Zapier Red Alert: Culture and Buildathon**

* Joined Zapier \~Jan 2026 to refound the marketing team for the agentic era  
* Problem: \~70% of the team couldn’t drive innovative value after AI tools arrived  
  * Common resistance: fear, “this isn’t my job,” “my craft is too important to outsource”  
* Called a “red alert”: brought entire 750-person remote company together in California  
  * Invited a bleeding-edge AI/marketing practitioner to present to the team  
  * Shock-and-awe moment: “you didn’t know this person existed, but you might interview against them”  
* Ran a 5-6 week buildathon immediately after  
  * First 3 weeks: everyone learned one dev tool (Cursor, Claude Code, Codex)  
  * Access to 5 power users on the team; no oversight on how much they engaged  
  * 60+ builds submitted; Dan reviewed every one with handwritten responses  
  * Three tiers emerged: beginner dashboards → full software platforms  
  * Outcome: team segmented by tier, paired across levels, fear replaced with momentum

#### **Marketing OS and Agentic Operations**

* Built a “Marketing OS”: single source of truth for the entire marketing team  
  * Aggregates signals from inside and outside Zapier daily  
  * Every morning: team reads what happened yesterday, goals status, opportunities  
  * Dan tracks who reads it; brand team initially resisted, quickly corrected  
* Found \$140K in expiring LinkedIn ad credits and 8,000 untracked ZapConnect signups  
* Runs same-day sales call analysis: wins/losses fed back for next day’s calls  
* “Lossless marketing”: prevents truth degradation as programs pass between teams  
* Slack transparency shift: moved from 49% public to \~100% public  
  * AI agents can only see public channels; private DMs are invisible to the OS  
  * Team target: 75% public; exec team graded monthly on their public/private ratio

#### **Team Builds, Org Redesign, and Compensation**

* Designer (Donna) built the entire ZapConnect conference management platform  
  * Cost savings in year one; revenue driver planned for year two  
* Rob Ayer (customer marketing) built an agentic system surfacing the right customer story for every sales call in real time  
* Matt Brown manages 150+ influencers as a team of one, fully agentic  
  * Real-time leaderboard tracking sign-ups, upgrades, and LTV (not impressions)  
  * Budget increasing 3x next year  
* Paid social fully automated: one person, end-to-end from use-case detection to ad push  
  * Brand team removed from approval loop; brand guidelines baked into the system  
* Measuring AI transformation via pipeline: “if we hit pipeline goals, the rest follows”  
  * Secondary metric: time to value for standing up agentic systems  
  * Qualitative signal: CEO feedback (“marketing is executing at a really high level”)  
* Org chart overhaul planned for 2027 planning cycle  
  * Rethinking where single-function roles live when one person runs an entire motion  
  * Hiring priorities: curious, flexible, resilient, multidisciplinary generalists  
  * Events flagged as a near-term growth area before an expected pullback  
* Experimenting with performance-based comp for marketers  
  * Employees who return headcount or generate pipeline should share in the upside  
  * Testing a percentage-of-SQL-pipeline model; finance is open to it  
  * “If a vendor offered \$10M pipeline for 2%, you’d sign immediately. Why not a person?”
