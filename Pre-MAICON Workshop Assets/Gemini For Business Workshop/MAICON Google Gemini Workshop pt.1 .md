## **Google’s AI Ecosystem: Known Frustrations**

* Google’s product naming and roadmap are notoriously inconsistent, even internally  
* Feature access varies between personal and business accounts, even on paid plans  
  * Some models and features only appear in personal accounts  
  * Exercises structured to work regardless of account type  
* Gems are being deprecated within \~6 months; Skills are the replacement  
* If a feature is missing: switch models, switch accounts, or move to the next exercise step

## **Gemini Capability Map**

* Reasoning models: Flash Lite (fast/light), Flash (balanced), Pro (slow/deep); match model to problem complexity  
* Voice input: 3–5x faster than typing; dictation button in Gemini web app; unlocks more context and nuance  
* Google Workspace integration: Gemini can read Docs, Sheets, Slides, Gmail, Meet; “Ask Gemini” tab in Workspace apps  
* Skills: repeatable prompt instructions (replacing Gems); unevenly rolled out  
* Scheduled actions: run prompts automatically on a schedule for recurring tasks  
* Image/video generation: built into Gemini; useful for marketing and visual ideation  
* Gemini in Chrome: can read web pages and interact with sites on your behalf  
* Gemini Spark / Tasks tab: emerging personal AI agent in personal accounts; agentic work across Google apps

## **Treating Gemini as a Strategic Thought Partner**

* Most users underuse Gemini for task execution (emails, docs); the real value is strategic consultation  
* Frame it as: expert advisor, second brain, 24/7 consultant available at no hourly rate  
* Approach: choose a real, hard business problem, not a generic content task  
* Use voice to pack in far more context, detail, and nuance than typing allows  
* Connect relevant Workspace files (Docs, Sheets, Slides) to give Gemini business context  
* Methodology: identify issues and drivers, explore framings, generate solutions, stress-test choices  
* Demo: B2B pipeline problem (“qualified opportunities not growing without increasing budget or headcount”)  
  * Gemini interviewed one question at a time before recommending anything  
  * Layered in context iteratively; separated supported facts from assumptions  
  * Exported full conversation to a Google Doc; used Gemini in Docs to create a project management tracker

## **Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)**

* A knowledge hub: add web pages, Docs, PDFs, YouTube videos, audio as sources; AI is automatically layered over all of them  
* Key use cases:  
  * Frequently referenced info: pricing, FAQs, campaign benchmarks, brand guidelines  
  * Onboarding and policy: always-on AI assistant with verified, up-to-date internal docs  
  * Customer insights: drop in call transcripts, survey responses; ask cross-customer questions instantly  
  * Fast learning: industry reports, conference notes, technical docs; query, compare, extract themes  
  * Existing content archives: blog posts, webinars, podcasts turned into a living knowledge base  
* Example: 200+ episode podcast archive on YouTube, all links dropped into Notebook; now queryable across years of content  
* Content generation from Notebook: audio overviews (podcast-style), video overviews, mind maps, slide decks, reports, quizzes, infographics  
* Notebooks can be used as sources directly within Gemini (via the \+ icon)  
* Deep Research reports can be run inside Notebook; sources auto-imported in one click  
* 5 sample notebooks shared in the workbook for different business use cases

## **Google AI Studio, Workspace Studio, and Antigravity**

* **AI Studio**: vibe-code apps through conversation; no coding required  
  * Build mode: plain-language prompt → working app, website, or tool  
  * Iterative: conversational editing to refine; add Gemini features (chat, voice, image gen) to apps  
  * Can connect to Gmail, Sheets, Docs, Drive, Calendar  
  * Demo: marketing budget planner built and iterated entirely via voice prompts  
  * Brian’s session Thursday goes deep on building marketing apps in AI Studio  
* **Workspace Studio**: AI workflow automation across Workspace apps (similar to Zapier/Make)  
  * Map sequential steps (draft doc → email → send); bake Gemini AI into workflow steps  
  * Access inconsistent; worth exploring if available  
* **Antigravity**: Google’s agentic system (comparable to Claude Code/Codex)  
  * Multi-agent, longer-horizon tasks; parallel agents on separate tasks  
  * More technical; many orgs don’t have it enabled yet

## **Use Case Prioritization Framework**

* Every job is a bundle of tasks; each task is a potential AI use case  
* Prioritize by two axes:  
  * Value: how much would improving this work actually matter?  
  * Feasibility: can you realistically do it with tools and information you already have?  
* High value \+ high feasibility \= start here; aim for 3–7 priority use cases  
* Match capabilities to use cases, not the other way around:  
  * Recurring workflows with clear steps → Workspace Studio  
  * Fuzzy goals needing exploration → agents (Antigravity)  
  * Strategic problems → Gemini as thought partner (Exercise 1\)  
  * Knowledge that needs to be reusable → Notebook  
  * Tools others need to use without knowing AI → AI Studio app  
* Build through conversation: describe what you want, iterate, refine; no perfect prompt required
