Five commitments: the four Dana read back at the end, plus Marcus agreeing to work on Priya's analysis ("I'd want Marcus on it." "Fine."), which her read-back left out.

## Raised, but deliberately not assigned

- **Who runs distributor onboarding day to day.** Dana asked Tom to own it. Tom said "I can own it," but wants to document it first and then decide who runs it. Dana accepted that order. Tom owns the process and will document it first. Who runs it day to day is still open.
- **A Q4 budget change.** Priya expects to ask for a reallocation, and possibly a content investment with a two-to-three-quarter payback. Nothing was decided. It comes back in October.
- **Placing buffer stock.** Not decided. It waits on Dana's Castellan read and Tom's model, and Marcus sees it before anything is placed.

## Raised, and not picked up

- **The downside if both Castellan deals slip.** Dana said that if both slip, the quarter ends about $400K short. Her call de-risks it, but nobody was asked for a plan if the answer is bad. Worth deciding whether that was deliberate.

## Flags

- **No commitment has a calendar date.** Two are relative ("this week," "end of next week"), which I've turned into Fridays counting from Monday 14 September. Check that's what people meant. The other three hang on the October meeting, and nobody gave its date.
- **Marcus's condition on Priya's analysis** ("send me the data before you build the analysis, not after") is included as part of her commitment. Priya didn't reply to it directly.
