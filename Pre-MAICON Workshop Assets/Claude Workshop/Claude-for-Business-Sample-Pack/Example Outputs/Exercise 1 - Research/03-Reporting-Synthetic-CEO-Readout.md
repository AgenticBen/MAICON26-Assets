# Channel readout, April to August 2026

## The finding

Organic search is shrinking and paid search is growing, but paid spend is rising faster than the revenue it brings in, at a return that has fallen every month, from 2.91 to 2.09. Monthly revenue is down 10.6 percent since April, from $458,200 to $409,800, while media spend rose from $43,300 to $73,500. Organic search alone lost $48,000 a month in revenue. Paid search added $29,600 a month, but only by adding $30,200 a month in spend. From July to August, paid spend went up $7,600 and paid revenue went up $3,000. My recommendation, starting now and carried into the Q4 budget, is to pull paid search spend back and find out why organic is falling before we spend more.

## The numbers behind it

**Paid search efficiency (revenue ÷ spend)**

| Month | Revenue | Spend | Revenue per $1 spent | Cost per conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| April | $119,800 | $41,200 | 2.91 | $208 |
| May | $132,600 | $48,900 | 2.71 | $221 |
| June | $141,600 | $57,300 | 2.47 | $243 |
| July | $146,400 | $63,800 | 2.29 | $261 |
| August | $149,400 | $71,400 | 2.09 | $287 |

Spend grew between 11 and 19 percent every month. Revenue grew between 2 and 11 percent. Spend grew faster than revenue in every month.

**Every channel, April to August (monthly revenue)**

| Channel | April | August | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic Search | $128,400 | $80,400 | −$48,000 (−37%) |
| Email | $105,600 | $84,600 | −$21,000 (−20%) |
| Direct | $72,600 | $63,600 | −$9,000 (−12%) |
| Social Organic | $13,200 | $5,400 | −$7,800 (−59%) |
| Paid Search | $119,800 | $149,400 | +$29,600 (+25%) |
| Referral | $18,600 | $26,400 | +$7,800 (+42%) |
| **All channels** | **$458,200** | **$409,800** | **−$48,400 (−11%)** |

Organic search sessions fell 27 percent, from 18,420 to 13,510. Paid search sessions rose 43 percent. Referral is the one unpaid channel that grew every month, on sessions, conversions and revenue. It is small.

## What I would do, and what it costs

1. **Take paid search spend back to June's level, $57,300 a month, from September, and budget Q4 at that level.** That saves $14,100 a month against August. Between June and August, that extra $14,100 bought $7,800 a month in revenue. If the relationship runs in reverse, we give up about $7,800 in revenue to save $14,100 in spend. That's an assumption, and September will test it before the Q4 number is locked.
2. **Find out why organic search is falling.** This file can't say why. It shows the drop, not the cause. It needs someone to go through search data at the page and query level: analyst time and no media spend (a few days is my estimate, not something in the data). Organic is the largest single loss.
3. **Put Email on the list after that.** It lost $21,000 a month, the second-largest drop, on $2,100 a month of spend.

Not recommended yet: moving budget into Referral. It is growing, but at $26,400 a month it can't cover a $48,000 gap.

## What this file can't tell us

- **Margin.** $2.09 of revenue for every dollar spent may or may not be profitable. That depends on gross margin, which isn't in the data.
- **Causes.** None of these numbers explain why organic, email or social declined.
- **Attribution.** Each conversion is counted in one channel. If paid search is picking up customers who used to arrive through organic, the paid numbers overstate what paid is adding.
