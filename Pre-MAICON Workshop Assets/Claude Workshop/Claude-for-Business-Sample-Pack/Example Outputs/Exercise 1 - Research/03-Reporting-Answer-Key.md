# Reporting and performance: sample material

Synthetic data for Northwind Supply, an invented industrial fastener distributor.
Five months of channel performance, April through August 2026.

## What is in the file

`03-Reporting-Channel-Performance-Data.csv` has one row per month per channel: sessions, engaged sessions,
conversions, revenue and spend. Thirty rows. Small enough to read, real enough to argue with.

## What is actually happening in it, in case you want to check your work

Organic search is falling steadily, down roughly twenty-seven percent in sessions across the
window. Paid search is growing and covering the gap on revenue, but spend is growing faster
than the revenue it returns. Paid search efficiency (Paid Search revenue divided by Paid Search spend) has gone from about 2.9 to about 2.1.
Social organic is collapsing. Email is quietly eroding. Referral is the only channel improving
on every measure, and it is small enough that nobody has noticed.

The interesting question is not "what happened." It is which of those five things a CEO needs
to hear first, and what you are recommending be done about it.
