## The brand voice note (what a style guide usually looks like before anyone fixes it)

Northwind Supply sells industrial fasteners to mid-market manufacturers. We have been
around since 1978. Our tone is "helpful expert." We are not stuffy. We avoid jargon where
possible but our buyers are engineers so some jargon is fine. Do not use exclamation points.
Do not say "revolutionary" or "game-changing." We say "customers," not "clients." We spell
out measurements. Our founder's name is Ruth Okafor and she still writes the December letter.

## Three published posts, lightly excerpted

### Post A: "Choosing the right thread pitch"
Thread pitch is one of those specifications that seems trivial until it isn't. A coarse
thread installs faster and tolerates a dirty hole. A fine thread holds better under
vibration and gives you more precise preload. Most of our customers over-specify fine
threads on assemblies that never see meaningful vibration, then wonder why install time
crept up. If your assembly is bolted once and inspected annually, coarse is almost always
the right answer. Ruth has been saying this for thirty years and the data agrees with her.

### Post B: "Why your fastener lead times moved"
Lead times on stainless moved from four weeks to nine over the last two quarters. Three
things drove it, and only one of them is going to resolve on its own. Mill capacity tightened
after two North American producers idled lines for maintenance in the same window. Freight
normalized but is still above 2019. And demand from the energy sector pulled inventory that
would ordinarily sit available. We are holding buffer stock on the fourteen SKUs our
customers order most, which covers roughly seventy percent of our order volume.

### Post C: "A note on substitutions"
When we substitute a part, we tell you before it ships. Every time. We have lost orders over
this and we are going to keep doing it, because the alternative is a customer discovering a
different coating on a part that is already installed in forty units. If you have ever had
that happen to you with another supplier, you understand why this is a hill worth standing on.
