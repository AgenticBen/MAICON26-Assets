# Meetings to actions: sample material

Synthetic transcript for the Claude for Business workshop. Northwind Supply is invented.
Names, numbers and decisions are all made up. Roughly twenty minutes of a real-feeling meeting.

---

**Q3 pipeline review. 14 September 2026. Present: Dana Whitfield (VP Sales), Marcus Reyes
(Finance), Priya Raman (Marketing), Tom Beckett (Operations).**

**DANA:** Let's start with the number. We're at 4.1 million closed against a 4.8 target, so
we're seven hundred short with six weeks left. That's recoverable but not comfortably.

**MARCUS:** Is that seven hundred in the pipeline or is that a gap we need to find?

**DANA:** Both, sort of. There's 2.2 in late stage. Historically we close about forty percent
of late stage in a quarter-end push, so call it eight eighty. That gets us there on paper.

**MARCUS:** On paper.

**DANA:** On paper. Two of those deals are the same customer and if they slip, they slip
together, and then we're four hundred short instead.

**PRIYA:** Which customer?

**DANA:** Castellan. The Ohio expansion and the retrofit contract. Same procurement person.

**PRIYA:** That feels like something we should de-risk rather than hope about.

**DANA:** Agreed. I'll take that. I'll get on a call with their procurement lead this week and
come back with a read on whether both land in Q3 or one moves.

**TOM:** If both land in Q3, can we actually ship them? Because the Ohio one is a hundred and
forty SKUs and stainless is still at nine weeks.

**DANA:** That's a fair question and I don't know the answer.

**TOM:** I'll model it. If both land I need to know by the end of next week, because buffer
stock decisions take a week to place.

**MARCUS:** Tom, if you're placing buffer stock against a deal that hasn't closed, I need to
see that before it goes out. That's working capital against a maybe.

**TOM:** Understood. I'll bring it to you with the model rather than just placing it.

**PRIYA:** Can I raise the organic traffic thing? It's not a Q3 issue but it's going to be a
Q4 one. We're down twenty-seven percent in sessions since April and paid is covering it, but
paid efficiency has gone from about 2.9 to about 2.1.

**MARCUS:** So we're buying the revenue we used to get for free.

**PRIYA:** Essentially. And the spend line is growing about eleven percent month over month to
hold the same revenue.

**MARCUS:** That is not sustainable into next year's budget.

**PRIYA:** No. I want to bring a proper analysis to the October meeting rather than hand-wave
at it now. I'd want Marcus on it so the efficiency numbers are ones finance recognizes.

**MARCUS:** Fine. Send me the data before you build the analysis, not after.

**DANA:** What's the actual ask in October? A budget change?

**PRIYA:** Probably a reallocation, and possibly a content investment that doesn't pay back
inside the quarter. I want to be honest that it's a two to three quarter payback.

**DANA:** Bring it with the payback stated. People can handle a long payback if you say so up
front.

**TOM:** One more thing, quickly. The distributor onboarding process. We had another account
go four months without a first order and nobody caught it. That's the third one this year.

**DANA:** Whose is that?

**TOM:** That's the problem. Nobody's. It depends on someone noticing.

**DANA:** Then let's give it an owner before we give it a process. Tom, can you own it?

**TOM:** I can own it. I'd rather document it first and then decide who runs it, because right
now there are two versions depending on who closed the deal.

**DANA:** Do it in that order then. Documented by the October meeting.

**MARCUS:** I have a hard stop.

**DANA:** We're done. Castellan read from me this week, shipping model from Tom by end of next
week with Marcus sighted before anything is placed, traffic analysis from Priya for October
with the payback stated, onboarding documented by Tom for October.
