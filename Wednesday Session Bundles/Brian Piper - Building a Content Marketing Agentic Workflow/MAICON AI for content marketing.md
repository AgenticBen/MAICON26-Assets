# **MAICON AI for content marketing**

Wed, 14 Oct 26

#### **Human in the Lead: The Core Framework**

* “Human in the lead sandwich”: human at top, not just in the loop  
  * Bottom bread: experience, knowledge, audience understanding, strategic goals  
  * Middle: AI handles research, brainstorming, drafting, optimization  
  * Top bread: human judgment, brand voice, final responsibility  
* AI trained on everything produces generic, beige content without human direction  
  * Passion, rationale, and personal stories don’t transfer automatically  
  * Hidden cost: time spent removing generic output, not creating

#### **Skills Over Prompts**

* Skills \= SOPs with guardrails and human checkpoints, refined over time  
  * Foundation for future autonomous agents  
  * Granular, reusable, improvable at every step  
* Orchestrator (master skill) runs all sub-skills in sequence  
  * Carries frameworks and guardrails through the entire process  
  * Logs a build journal for iteration and friction analysis  
* AI interviews you to pull the story out, then drafts using your words  
  * Inspired by Jeff Woods’ Crick framework  
  * Checks alignment with brand and audience at each stage  
* Build in a skeptic: explicitly instruct the tool to critique, push back, and find weak arguments

#### **The Agentic Content Engine**

* Data insight skill: six audits (strategic, audience, analytics, performance, information architecture, search)  
  * Reduced from 3-4 weeks to \~3 hours; automated weekly email with insights and actions  
* Ideas skill: daily email scanning newsletters, publications, and trusted influencers  
* Storybook: database of tagged customer and expertise stories, trackable by performance  
* Personas: fed by real customer support data and sales transcripts, not guesswork  
* Discoverability baked in at every step: SEO, GEO, and future agent optimization (AEO)  
* Research: run prompts across four models, compare outputs in NotebookLM, audio debate to select what to include  
* Collaborate skill: proposes hooks, frameworks, outline; tests against audience and goals  
* Critique skill: finds gaps, challenges weak arguments, recommends edits (doesn’t make them)  
* Guardrails: checks for bias, hallucinations, duplicate content, and regulatory compliance  
* Four Rs of repurposing: Repurpose, Retarget, Redistribute, Retire  
* Traffic tracking: logs parent content, channel, brand, AI usage level, and entry source  
* Channel playbook: per-channel skill that updates based on latest algorithm changes
