# **MAICON \- AI Optimization that builds credibility**

Wed, 14 Oct 26

#### **AI Visibility vs. Recommendability**

* Being visible in AI results does not mean being recommended  
  * AI can surface a brand and simultaneously signal distrust or warn against it  
  * Vacation rental client example: showed up in results but was actively anti-recommended  
* Rand Fishkin study: same prompt run 14-29 times produces different brand rankings each time  
  * Google-era assumption (visible \= recommended) is broken in AI world

#### **GEO 1.0 vs. 2.0: The Loophole Trap**

* GEO 1.0 rewarded “best” and “top” keyword stuffing; brands gamed it and won short-term  
* GEO 2.0 uses query fan-out: AI searches by brand names it already trusts, not generic superlatives  
  * Brands that gamed 1.0 can’t outrank established brands for their own name  
  * Banana Republic ranked for “ethical” by keyword stuffing; AI now ignores that signal  
* Loophole strategies produce volatile traffic (spike then crash); credibility strategies produce stable growth  
* AI companies have unlimited compute to detect and penalize pattern-based manipulation  
* Vetting vendor case studies: drop their client’s domain into Semrush; if organic traffic fell, the strategy is the same one being sold to you

#### **Building Real Receipts: Humans First**

* AI’s brand beliefs are hardwired the same way human brand associations are (e.g., “luxury hotel” → Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton instantly)  
  * No single content piece hardwired those brands; sustained presence did  
* Receipts that matter to AI: coverage in trusted industry publications (Search Engine Land, Search Engine Journal), endorsement from recognized thought leaders (Rand Fishkin, Lily Ray, Cyrus Shepard)  
* Cross-functional coordination is now the \#1 marketer skill: PR, brand, PPC, sales, and content must reinforce the same claims  
  * AI checks whether your stated expertise is corroborated across sources  
* Trust signals AI now surfaces: third-party review platforms (e.g., Capterra for B2B software); SEO alone won’t get a brand listed there  
* Conversion data supporting the human-first approach:  
  * Direct/dark social traffic converts 2.5x better than search or AI  
  * Social converts 6x better than search or AI  
  * Referral from trusted publications outperforms search and AI

#### **Actionable Prompts and Tactics**

* Analyze sales call transcripts to surface customer language not reflected on the website or in tracked prompts  
  * Prompt: *“What words or concerns are showing up in sales calls that are not in my sales materials or on my website?”*  
  * Prompt: *“What words are my customers using that are not being tracked in my prompts?”*  
* Audit content for visibility without human engagement  
  * Prompt: *“What keywords am I ranking for in SEO/GEO where I have little evidence I’m truly a leader in that space?”*  
  * Prompt: *“What is similar about the high-traffic content I produce that gets little direct referral or social traffic?”*  
* Monitor AI platform blogs via RSS; cross-reference with client performance data to spot optimization opportunities  
* Use Phantom Buster to scrape thought leader posts and check whether your brand is being mentioned  
* Track brand mentions as a primary KPI, not just visibility rankings
