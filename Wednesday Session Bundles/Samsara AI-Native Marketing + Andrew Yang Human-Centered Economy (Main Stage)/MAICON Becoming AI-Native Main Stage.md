# **MAICON Becoming AI-Native Main Stage** 

Wed, 14 Oct 26

#### **Making Every Marketer AI-Native (Samsara Case Study)**

* Samsara: \$2B ARR connected operations platform  
* 100+ AI agents in production across marketing, 200+ personal productivity agents  
* 70% built by non-technical marketers (event, content, growth)  
* Went from 3% to 90% of marketers building AI workflows in \~one year

#### **The Three-Part Framework**

* Make them start: leadership sets expectation, CEO makes AI a company priority, every manager reports on team usage  
* Make them capable: skills, time, and access  
  * AI Builder Bootcamp: 3-week program, two nominees per team, graduation project required  
  * Graduates return as team evangelists (people learn faster from peers)  
  * Hackathons: 3-person teams, 4 weeks to build, present to leadership on-site  
  * AI Power 30: 30-minute monthly hands-on training for the whole team  
* Make it stick: structure and habit  
  * AI in performance reviews company-wide  
  * Standing “AI in Action” slot in every monthly all-hands  
  * AI champions per team, central registry, cost tracking

#### **Tool Adoption Curve**

* Oct: Gumloop introduced (workflow only), \~3% adoption  
* March: personal agent workshop, jumped to \~50% building  
* June: Claude introduced, felt familiar rather than new  
  * Each tool made the next easier to adopt  
* July: 90%+ marketers building

#### **Real-World Agent Examples**

* Demand gen agent: pulls qualified direct mail contact lists via Slack request, runs 7 layers of qualification, verifies employment and address; 140 lists generated in 2 months  
* Competitive ad feature: scrapes competitor ads weekly, identifies what works, suggests creative ideas, posts to Slack every Monday  
* Mexico team (8 people) built 14 of the 40 agents in the growth team alone  
* ABM program: scaled from 10 to 26 accounts per quarter with the same headcount  
* Results: 30% more pipeline per marketing dollar YoY, 50% more pipeline per marketing head YoY

#### **Scaling Challenges and Governance**

* Four problems at scale: quality, duplication, maintenance, cost  
  * Cost jumps in waves at each tool rollout, not linearly  
  * GONG MCP example: reorganizing data ahead of queries cut costs 99%  
* Govern by tier, not lockdown:  
  * Personal agents: no approval needed  
  * Team agents: reviewed and hosted in central repository  
  * Org agents: built and maintained by central team  
* A prototype is not a product: once others rely on an agent, it needs documentation and an owner  
* “Scratch test” norm: if it feels like AI did it, it’s not done

#### **The Future: Jobs, Not Agents**

* 40 agents in growth team map to just 6 distinct jobs to be done  
* Prediction: the unit of work shifts from agents to jobs  
  * Marketers define the job, the system assembles the work  
* Orchestration layer is the missing piece (“the dispatch”)  
  * Agents are drivers, data is roads, dispatch assigns the right driver to the right job  
* Next year’s challenge: build the orchestration and governance layer, not more agents

#### **Andrew Yang: AI, Labor, and a Human-Centered Economy**

* AI built on collective data now powering trillion-dollar valuations; individuals got little in return  
* 49% of US jobs concentrated in 5 sectors (clerical, retail, food service, trucking, manufacturing), all highly exposed  
  * 2.8M call center workers; Verizon bots already showing 13% higher satisfaction than humans  
* Knowledge premium for college graduates shrinking fast; \~25% of private colleges may close in 5-10 years  
* AI bubble concern: capital hyper-allocated to AI, echoes of the first internet bubble  
* Social effects: declining dating, marriage, birth rates; AI relationships offering frictionless substitutes for human ones  
* Vision for a human-centered economy:  
  * Tax bots, not workers; distribute value broadly as a dividend floor  
  * Build new economic value around art and creativity, health and wellness, caring and nurturing
